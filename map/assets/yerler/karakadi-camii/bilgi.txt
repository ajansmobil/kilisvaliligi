Kara Kadi Camii is located in
Cumhuriyet Street.
Kadıoğlu ve Karakadı isimleriyle de bilinen Kadı Camii, “Karakadı” olarak
bilinen Ali bin Ahmet Alaeddin er-Rumi tarafından yaptırılmıştır. 16. yy’dan bu
yana hizmet veren cami başlangıçta kare planlı olarak yapılsa da yıllar boyu
yapılan onarımlarla dikdörtgen bir plana evrilmiştir.
Kadi Mosque, also know with the names of Kadioglu and Karakadi, was built by
Ali Bin Alaeddin El Rumi, who is also known as Karakadi, too. The mosque, built
in the 16th century, was first planned in a square floor but, after restorations
and time, it evolved into a rectangle structure.
