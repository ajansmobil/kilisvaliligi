yapıdır. Cumhuriyet caddesinin girişinde bulunan cami, 1978 yılında Kilisli

Kilis’ first two-minareted mosque is Haci Cumbus Mosque and it also has
surrounding next to it. The mosque, located in the entrance to Cumhuriyet
Street, built by a Kilis fellow philanthropist businessman in 1978.
