Sokağı’nda bulunmaktadır.
It is found in Old Hammam Road,
Meshedlik Neighbourhood.
Eski Hamam, Kilis’in geleneksel hamam mimarisini yansıtan önemli tarihi
yapılarından biridir. Taş işçiliği, plan özellikleri ve döneminin mimari izlerini taşıyan
yapısıyla dikkat çeken hamam, günümüzde restoran olarak kullanılmaktadır.
Eski Hamam (Old Bathhouse) is one of the important historical structures
reflecting the traditional bathhouse architecture of Kilis. With its stone
craftsmanship, spatial layout, and architectural features of its period, it stands
out as a notable example of the city’s heritage. Today, the building is used as
a restaurant, giving it a new function while preserving its historical character.
