Being the second oldest structure in Kilis after Ulu Mosque, Cuneyye Mosque
was subjected to Evliya Celebi’s Journal. Evliya Celebi mentioned about a
dome but, as some of the parts have lost their originality, it does not exist
today, although the minaret is still in its original shape.
