Bey and completed in 1925, is found in city centre. It served as the mayor office for 70 
years and then as courthouse for 18 years, ans has been serving as the government 
office since 2015.

since 1954. Today, it functions as a Baby and Children’s Library, contributing 
to children’s cultural development with its reading areas, children’s activities, 
and cinema section.
